Ever wonder how much value the talents you [i]didn't[/i] pick would have given you? This mod shows you running totals for your current run. For each talent in a row, it displays how much that talent [i]would have[/i] provided based on your real hits this run, whether or not you have it equipped. Each talent row gets its own on-screen panel that you can toggle, drag, and reset.
[b]Most accurate when you are the host.[/b]
[hr][/hr]
[h2]Temp Health (Level 5)[/h2]
Shows how much temporary health each Level 5 talent would have generated for you this run:
[list]
[*][b]Sting[/b] — from your first-target melee hits
[*][b]Carve[/b] — from cleaving through multiple targets
[*][b]Execute[/b] — from your melee kills
[*][b]Second Wind[/b] — from staggering enemies
[/list]
So you can see at a glance which THP talent would actually have kept you healthiest for [i]your[/i] playstyle this run.
Optional extra columns: how much of that healing would have [b]decayed[/b] away unused, what [b]percentage of the time[/b] each talent's healing would be actively decaying, and how much real [b]damage[/b] each talent's healing would have absorbed.
[hr][/hr]
[h2]Stagger Talents (Level 15)[/h2]
Shows the extra melee damage each Level 15 stagger talent would have added this run, as [b]Total[/b] and [b]First-target[/b] running totals:
[list]
[*][b]Mainstay[/b] — bonus damage vs. staggered / multi-staggered enemies
[*][b]Smiter[/b] — first enemy hit counts as staggered
[*][b]Bulwark[/b] — enemies you stagger take extra melee damage for a few seconds
[*][b]Assassin[/b] — bonus damage vs. staggered, boosted on crits/headshots
[*][b]Enhanced Power[/b] — a flat Power Level boost affecting damage, cleave, and stagger
[/list]
A [b]Force Enhanced Power[/b] option can enable the extra cleave that Enhanced Power would grant, so you can see the additional enemies and damage it would physically hit.

[h2]About Attack Speed Estimations[/h2]
Some talents (like the Mercenary Level 20 row) grant [b]bonus attack speed[/b], which is hard to value directly. This mod estimates it with a "ghost swing" model: it takes your real swings this run and simulates the extra swings you [i]would have[/i] landed at the higher attack speed, valuing each ghosted swing at the damage of a real one. Because this assumes you'd have kept swinging non-stop, these attack-speed numbers are an [b]upper-bound estimate[/b], not an exact figure, treat them as "best case" value.

[h2]Tourney Balance Support[/h2]
If the [b]Tourney Balance[/b] mod is loaded, the panels automatically switch to Tourney Balance's reworked talent numbers so the comparisons stay accurate.
