[h1]How the estimates work[/h1]

[b]Stagger-number model:[/b] every hit's damage secretly depends on a hidden "stagger number." The mod reverses that math on each real hit to recover the base damage, then reapplies each talent's own stagger rule to see what that hit would have done instead.

[b]Power recompute:[/b] for talents that boost Power Level, the mod re-runs the real hit's damage formula with the talent's power bonus added in, then reports the difference — same enemy, same hit, just with/without the bonus.

[b]Kill tracker:[/b] tracks two running totals per enemy — damage with vs. without a given talent — and checks whether the "with" total would have killed the enemy sooner than reality did. Results are scaled against the real damage the enemy actually took, so they stay accurate even though the underlying math runs on a pre-final damage number.

[b]Attack-speed simulation ("ghost swings"):[/b] for talents that speed up attacks rather than hit harder, the mod replays your real swing timeline at the faster rate and counts how many extra swings would have landed in that time, valuing each ghost swing at the same damage as a real nearby swing. This is an upper bound — it assumes every extra swing would have connected.

[b]Forward projection:[/b] for damage-over-time effects that can't be safely applied to your character, the mod takes one real tick's damage and projects a full duration/tick-rate timeline forward from it, stopping at the enemy's remaining health. This is also an upper bound — it assumes the projected ticks all land.

[b]Simulated cooldown:[/b] for cooldown-refund talents, the mod runs a private, invisible copy of your ability cooldown per talent, fed by your real combat pace but with the other talent's effects stripped out. This keeps each talent's number independent of which one you actually have equipped.

[b]Cross-mod estimate:[/b] when comparing against another mod's rebalanced talents, real observed events (hits, staggers, crits) are re-scored using that mod's formulas. Where a value depends on a hidden game number that a loaded mod can silently change (e.g. weapon impact/stagger strength), the cross-mod value is a labeled estimate rather than an exact figure.
